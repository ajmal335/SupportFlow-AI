
CUSTOMER SUPPORT - RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

1. RETURN PERIOD
Customers can request a return within 30 days
after the product delivery date.

2. PRODUCT CONDITION
Products must be unused and in their original
packaging to qualify for a return.

3. REFUND ELIGIBILITY
A refund can only be considered after checking
the order details and return eligibility.

4. SHIPPING STATUS
Orders that have not been delivered cannot
be processed as normal return requests.

5. REFUND APPROVAL
Every refund request must be reviewed and
approved by a human before processing.

6. REFUND METHOD
Approved refunds will be returned through
the original payment method.

7. FINAL DECISION
The customer support agent must never process
a refund without human approval.
